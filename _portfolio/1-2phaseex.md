Turning unused heat produced by data centers into a resource. Development of a closed-loop, two-phase heat exchanger based on the thermosiphon principle, using environmentally friendly fluids for thermal energy recovery. Industrial partners include 2CRSI, BDR Thermea, SOGEFI and Secon.
